      Common questions

      Do I need a prescription?

      Some medicines, such as antibiotics, are only given with a prescription from a doctor. Many others you can buy without one. If you are not sure, ask us.

      How do I pay?

      We confirm your total on WhatsApp first. You then pay in store, by bank transfer or on delivery.

      Do you deliver?

      Ask for delivery when you order. We will tell you if we can deliver to your area and confirm the fee and time with you.

      Can I return a medicine?

      For your safety, we cannot take back medicines once they leave the pharmacy, unless we made a mistake. Check your order before you leave or when it arrives.

      How do refill reminders work?

      You enter the medicine and how many days it lasts. The reminder is saved in your browser on this device and shows when a refill is due. It does not send messages.
